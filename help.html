Alarm didn't ring?

Android · Português

When an alarm doesn't ring on Android, the cause is almost always the phone's battery manager. Many makers add their own battery saving on top of Android, and it can stop apps that run in the background. When it stops Wake at Sunrise, it can take the alarm with it.

The app helps with this: it asks for the battery exemption on first launch, shows a banner on phones from the makers below, and, if it notices a missed alarm, explains what to change. This page has the same steps in one place.

On every Android phone

  1. App info → Battery: choose Unrestricted (on some phones: turn off battery optimization for Wake at Sunrise).
  2. Allow notifications for the app. The alarm rings through a notification.
  3. Don't swipe the app away from the recent apps list.

Samsung

  1. Battery → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps: add Wake at Sunrise.
  2. App info → Battery: choose Unrestricted.

Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco

  1. Autostart: turn it on for Wake at Sunrise.
  2. Battery saver: choose No restrictions.

OPPO, realme, OnePlus

  1. App info → Battery: turn on Allow background activity and Allow auto launch.
  2. Recent apps: lock the app by pulling its card down.

vivo, iQOO

  1. Autostart (or Background startup): allow Wake at Sunrise.
  2. Battery → Background power consumption: allow high usage.

Huawei, Honor

  1. App launch: turn off Manage automatically for Wake at Sunrise.
  2. Allow Auto-launch, Secondary launch and Run in background.

TECNO, Infinix, itel

  1. Phone Master → Auto-start: allow Wake at Sunrise.
  2. Battery → App power saving: turn it off for Wake at Sunrise.

Menu names can differ slightly between system versions. In the app, the banner's Fix button opens the right screen on most of these phones.

Still not ringing?

Email wakeatsunriseapp [at] gmail [dot] com with your phone model. The app reports missed alarms anonymously, without your location, which helps find the cause.
