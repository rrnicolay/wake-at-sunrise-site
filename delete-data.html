Request data deletion

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Wake at Sunrise is an Android alarm app published by Rafael Nicolay. It has no accounts and no sign-up. This page explains how to delete the data associated with it, and what happens to each kind of data.

Delete everything stored on your phone

Settings, the cached coordinates used for the sunrise calculation, and the local technical log are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of them immediately — no request needed, and nothing of that kind exists anywhere else.

Request deletion of data held by our processors

Two kinds of technical data may exist outside your device: crash reports and purchase records (see the table below). To have them deleted:

  1. Send an email to wakeatsunriseapp [at] gmail [dot] com with the subject "Data deletion request — Wake at Sunrise".
  2. Say what you want deleted: crash reports, purchase records, or both.
  3. For purchase records, include the Google Play order ID of the purchase (Play Store app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history), or the email address of the Google account used to buy. Without it, the purchase cannot be identified, because the app never learns who you are.
  4. For crash reports, include the approximate date and your device model, or simply ask for all reports from your device to be removed.

Requests are acknowledged within 7 days and completed within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataOn deletion request
On-device settings, cached coordinates, local log Not applicable — removed by uninstalling the app. Never transmitted anywhere.
Crash reports and diagnostics (processed by Sentry) Deleted on request. Also expire automatically about 90 days after they are received.
Purchase records (processed by RevenueCat) Deleted on request. Note that deleting them means Premium can no longer be restored automatically on a new device.
Transaction records held by Google Play Retained by Google as the seller of record, for the period required by tax and accounting law. These are outside the developer's control; requests concerning them go to Google Play support.
