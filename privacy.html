Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Wake at Sunrise is an Android alarm app that rings relative to the local sunrise time. It is built to work without collecting personal data. This page describes exactly what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your location is used on your device only, to compute sunrise times. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Two kinds of technical data can leave your device: anonymous crash reports when the app hits an error, and purchase records when you buy the optional Premium upgrade. Neither includes your location.

Location

The app asks for your device's location for a single purpose: calculating the sunrise time at your position. The calculation is performed entirely on your device using astronomical formulas — no map service, weather service, or server is contacted. Your coordinates are stored locally on your device so the alarm keeps working offline, and they are never included in any network transmission.

Crash reports

Official builds of the app include error reporting (via Sentry) so that crashes can be found and fixed. These reports are sent only when an error occurs and contain technical details about the failure (such as the error message, stack trace, app version, and device model). They are configured to exclude personally identifying information, and location coordinates are deliberately never attached to them.

Purchases (Premium)

The app offers an optional paid upgrade ("Premium"), sold through Google Play as an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. To validate purchases and restore Premium across reinstalls and devices, purchase records — the product bought, timestamps, and a randomly generated anonymous app-user ID — are processed by RevenueCat, a purchase-infrastructure provider, and by Google Play. Payment itself is handled entirely by Google Play: the app and its developer never see your payment details. If you never buy anything, no purchase data exists.

Data sharing and selling

No data is ever sold, and nothing is shared with third parties beyond the two processors described above: Sentry (crash reports) and RevenueCat (purchase records). To request deletion of either, use the contact option below.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects your data, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before that version ships.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores on your phone is removed by uninstalling it. For crash reports and purchase records held by the processors above, see how to request data deletion.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Use the in-app "Send Feedback" option, or reach the developer via the contact link on the home page.
